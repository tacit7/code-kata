Burst Balloons — Interval DP. Pad nums with boundary 1s. dp[left][right] = max coins from bursting all balloons strictly between left and right. For each window, try every k as the last balloon to burst — its neighbors are the boundary values left and right (still intact). Fill by increasing window size. Answer is dp[0][n+1].
